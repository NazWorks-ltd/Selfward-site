Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version. The Selfward app keeps everything you enter on your phone. We don't have accounts or servers for the app, and we don't use analytics, ads or tracking.

If you sign up for the beta on this website, we keep the details you give us so we can send you a TestFlight invite and beta updates. That's all we use them for.

This policy explains how NazWorks Ltd ("we", "us") handles information in the Selfward iPhone app and on iamselfward.com.

The Selfward app

What the app stores

To work, the app saves what you enter:

  • your "I am…" identity and the themes you pick
  • your routines, habits, pathways and stacks
  • check-ins, including any check marks you draw
  • reminder times and your settings, like the app's look

All of this is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no copy of it and can't see it.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party tools that collect your data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health data or microphone access.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your phone using iOS notifications. They're created on your device and don't go through any server of ours.

Backups

Like most apps, Selfward's data can be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or your computer), depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple and controlled by you.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Start over in the app to erase your identity, habits and history, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

The TestFlight beta

Beta versions of Selfward are distributed through Apple's TestFlight. When you test the app, Apple may share information with us, such as the email address you were invited with, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix problems and improve Selfward. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

This website and the beta signup

Beta signup form

The signup form asks for your email address, and optionally your first name and a few short questions about your iPhone and your habits. We use your answers only to:

  • send your TestFlight invite
  • send updates and occasional feedback requests about the beta
  • decide who to invite in each batch

The form is provided by Tally, which stores the responses for us. See Tally's privacy policy. Emails we send you come from our Google Workspace account.

Browsing the site

We don't use analytics or set cookies on this website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the page, under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We don't sell your information or share it for advertising. We only share it with the service providers named above, to run the beta and this website.

How long we keep it

We keep beta signup details until the beta ends or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the signup information we hold about you, or to stop emailing you, at any time. Email peter@nazworks.net. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children

Selfward isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has signed up for the beta, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date above. If a change affects how the app handles your data, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
